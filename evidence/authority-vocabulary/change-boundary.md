# Authority-vocabulary proof boundary

## Before

The transitional runtime still mounted TrialScope renderers and defaulted to
its old chat route. A representative ten-panel render could not prove the
production registry itself was closed.

## Intended after

Prove the production Assistant mounts only the exact ten NodeQuant panels and
defaults to `/api/nodequant-chat`, with a real backend review returning 200.
Then render one strict representative payload for each registered panel through
the production data-part renderer on one 1100px page. Assert each panel test ID
occurs exactly once, no unknown panel appears, and every panel fits its container.
`research-diagram` must remain visibly dashed, quarantined, and explicitly carry
no facts, figures, or verdicts.

## Out of scope

- Representative payloads prove renderer vocabulary and visual authority cues,
  not market or trading claims.
- The temporary intercept harness is evidence-only and is removed after capture.
- This artifact does not clear the review gate or any promotion blocker.
