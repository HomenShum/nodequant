Overlap disclosure

The signature component. ClinicalTrials.gov stores phases as an array, so one trial can occupy several phase buckets. Buckets therefore do not partition the result set and must never be drawn as a pie or a 100% stacked bar. This strip states that in the reader's line of sight.

State: buckets overlap

≠

These buckets overlap and do not sum to the total.

A trial may be registered under more than one phase, so it is counted in each. 469 trials are tagged both PHASE1 and PHASE2.

bucket sum 3,205 ≠ trials matching 2,915

Rendered whenever meta.buckets_are_exclusive === false. Not a tooltip and not fine print — a reader who misses this misreads every proportion on the page.

State: buckets are exclusive

=

Buckets are mutually exclusive.

Each trial appears in exactly one bucket, so shares of the total are safe to read and part-of-whole marks are permitted.

bucket sum 2,862= trials matching 2,862
