Most melanoma trials never reach Phase 3.

Of 3,738 registered melanoma trials, 1,529 are Phase 2 and just 219 are Phase 3 — but the phases do not divide cleanly, and that is the more interesting fact.

ClinicalTrials.gov stores phases as a list, so a single trial can occupy several bars at once. These bars therefore cannot be read as slices of a whole — they sum to 3,602, while 3,738 trials matched. A further 136 carry no phase field at all.

This is why the chart above is drawn as independent bars rather than a pie. A part-of-whole mark would imply a partition that the underlying data does not have, and every proportion a reader took from it would be wrong.
