TrialScope foundations

Tokens for a clinical evidence surface. Every choice is load-bearing; the grey ramp is reserved and may not be spent on decoration.

Colour

One accent hue carries all measured data. Grey means exactly one thing: no value was recorded. Amber marks a claim the reader must temper. Nothing else may use these three roles.

--accentmeasured data
--accent-weaksecondary series
--nullunbucketed only
--warndisclosure
--inktext
--mutedsecondary text

Numerals

All counts use tabular figures so columns align and digit changes do not reflow. Counts are never abbreviated — 1,742 not 1.7k, because a truncated count cannot be checked against the source.

Accessibility floor

  • Never encode meaning by colour alone — every bar also carries a numeric label.
  • The unbucketed category is distinguished by label and hatch, not only by grey.
  • Focus rings are never removed; disclosure controls are real buttons.
